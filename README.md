AI Mental Health Companion
📌 Problem Statement
Students and professionals often struggle with mental anxiety and pressure caused by academic challenges or career-related stress. These issues can negatively impact productivity, emotional well-being, and overall quality of life. Our team wanted to address this growing concern by building a supportive, AI-powered solution.

💡 Why We Built This
During Hackmanthon 2025, organized by EventEye in collaboration with GeeksforGeeks at Lovely Professional University (LPU), our team set out to create a platform that combines technology with empathy. The goal was to provide accessible mental health support, reduce stress, and offer tools for emotional resilience.

🚀 Features
AI Chatbot with sentiment analysis to detect emotions and respond empathetically.
Games and relaxation techniques including yoga practices to help users relieve pressure.
One-on-one support feature connecting users with real psychiatrists for professional guidance.
Crisis prediction and voice analysis modules to assess well-being and escalate when needed.
Analytics dashboard for tracking emotional trends and therapy outcomes.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js
Backend: Flask, Python, REST API
Database: SQLite
AI/ML: TensorFlow, PyTorch, spaCy, NumPy, Pandas
Version Control: Git

🏆 Hackathon Context
This project was developed in October–November 2025 as part of Hackmanthon 2025. It reflects our commitment to leveraging AI for social good and creating tools that can make a real difference in people’s lives.
